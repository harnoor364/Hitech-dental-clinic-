06 Patient FAQ

Questions patients often ask

Have a question that isn't listed here? Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll happily answer it.

Is root canal treatment (RCT) painful?

RCT is done under local anaesthesia, so the tooth and the area around it are numb. Most people feel only mild pressure. Many of our patients describe their root canal as painless, and Dr. Param explains every step before she begins.

How long does tooth capping take?

Usually two visits. At the first, the tooth is prepared and measured. At the second, a few days later, the final cap is fitted. The exact timing depends on your tooth and the cap material.

Does tooth extraction hurt?

The area is numbed before the extraction, so you should feel pressure rather than pain. You will get clear aftercare instructions for a comfortable recovery.

Do you treat pigmentation?

Yes. The clinic offers pigmentation and skin treatments. Each one starts with a skin assessment so the plan suits your skin type and concern.
